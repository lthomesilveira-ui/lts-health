The owner requests a real comparison of the stable and public-preview Google Health channels after the new original export was reviewed. The current production frontend and automatic stable reader remain unchanged. This package adds a separate authenticated, explicitly requested evaluation, with the existing encrypted grant and owner lease.

Both channels use the same identity, read-only nutrition grant, physical time interval in Sao Paulo, source-family selection and bounded page size. The result distinguishes positive volumes, explicit zero, unverifiable local dates, pagination and unavailable operations. It exposes no amount, source identifier, point payload, account identifier or credential. No snapshot is created and no canonical clinical row is written.
